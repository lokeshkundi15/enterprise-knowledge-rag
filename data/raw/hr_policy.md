# Global Enterprise HR Policies 2026

## 1. Parental Leave Policy
Employees are eligible for up to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Employees must provide at least 30 days advance notice to the HR department via the internal Workday portal. Health insurance benefits continue uninterrupted during the leave period.

## 2. Remote Work & Home Office Stipend
Full-time remote employees are eligible for a one-time home office equipment stipend of $750 upon joining. Receipts must be submitted through Concur within 60 days of purchase. Allowed items include ergonomic chairs, external monitors, and sit-stand desks.

## 3. 401(k) Retirement Match
The company matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to the first 4% of eligible base salary, and 50% on the next 2%, resulting in a maximum total employer match of 5%. Vesting occurs immediately upon contribution.
